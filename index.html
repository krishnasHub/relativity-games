RELATIVITY GAMES

Small browser games about special and general relativity: time dilation, Doppler shifts, aberration, the twin paradox, and eventually black holes. The physics is computed for real, and each game's Info screen says what was tuned to make it playable.

Proper Time

Pilot a courier at 0.9999c from the cockpit. Watch the sky squeeze into a starbow, beacons shift out of sight, and your twin age an hour while you age a minute. Keyboard and mouse.
